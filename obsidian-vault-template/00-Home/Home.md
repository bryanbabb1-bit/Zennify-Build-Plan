## Active Clients

> Use the folder `01-Clients/` — one subfolder per client.

## How This Vault Connects to the Tool

When you complete discovery for a client, copy the relevant notes from `01-Clients/[Client]/03-Discovery/` and paste them directly into the **Zennify Build Planner** as Discovery Notes input. The AI will use them to generate the project plan.

Standards documents in `02-Standards/` mirror the **Standards Documents** section in the admin panel of the tool. Keep them in sync.
